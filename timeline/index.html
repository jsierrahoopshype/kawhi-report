Timeline of the Kawhi Leonard / Clippers investigation

Download the report (PDF)

Every dated event in the report, from the 2015 DeAndre Jordan fine to the day the findings were released. Threads: the three 2020 deals, Aspiration and the Forum, the investigation itself, and background.

DateThreadEvent
2015BackgroundDeAndre Jordan fine
The Clippers try to facilitate an endorsement between Jordan and an incoming sponsor during his free agency. The NBA fines them $250,000. Ballmer: "any circumvention was inadvertent."
July 2019BackgroundKawhi signs with the Clippers
During free agency Robertson asks multiple teams for equity, housing, private transportation and guaranteed endorsement income. The Clippers acknowledge the requests to the NBA but deny agreeing to them. The league leaves its file open.
Summer-fall 2019BackgroundNBA rules enforcement initiative
As a direct result of Robertson's conduct, the NBA adopts a rule requiring teams to report any player solicitation of unauthorized benefits, even if rejected.
Within months of the July 2019 signing2020 dealsRobertson sets a target
Robertson resumes his pressure and communicates that he expects the Clippers' help obtaining approximately $10M per year for Leonard, primarily to Frank but also to Ballmer and Zucker. The report finds no evidence anyone told him to stop or reported it.
Dec 4, 2019BackgroundCircumvention training at the Clippers
The league trains Ballmer, Zucker, Frank and other staff on the rules, in the same period as the misconduct.
April 20202020 dealsRobertson vents to Ballmer and Frank
Per Frank's contemporaneous notes: Zucker is making intros for "bull**** deals," "I have to get paid." Ballmer replies they are "collective workers to try to help [Leonard] achieve his financial goals." Robertson asks for a 3-6 month plan and a pipeline of 5-6 companies.
May 20202020 dealsDaktronics gets the terms
The Clippers tell Daktronics it is the preferred Intuit Dome scoreboard provider but want a "spend back." Zucker suggests a Kawhi endorsement. A senior Clippers executive specifies $3M per year for two years.
May 2020BackgroundBallmer buys the Forum
The Inglewood arena, overseen by Zucker, later becomes the vehicle for the Aspiration business-back deal.
Early June 20202020 dealsThree intro emails in six days
Zucker connects Robertson with executives at Boingo, Daktronics and Lockton. Each email is written to appear responsive to a request from the company. The report says no documentary evidence supports that, the evidence on one company contradicts it, and investigators do not credit that all three asked within six days during the pandemic shutdown.
June 9, 20202020 dealsKL2 LBS LLC is filed
Same day as the second intro email. Members: Leonard and Robertson. The report notes this was before any negotiations could have begun if the introduction chronology were genuine. KL2 becomes the counterparty on all three 2020 deals.
Early July 20202020 dealsTwo deals signed the same day
Daktronics signs July 6. One of Boingo or Lockton signs the same day; the report does not say which. Each company also enters a multi-million dollar consulting agreement with the Clippers, two of them paid $10M up front.
By end of August 20202020 dealsThird deal signed
The remaining company signs. By early September Leonard has been paid under all three agreements.
Feb-May 20212020 dealsDaktronics told to pay $2M more
The same Clippers executive: the team is spending more on the scoreboard, so raise Kawhi's pay. Amendment executed May 20, 2021.
Aug 20212020 dealsAll $18M paid; Kawhi re-signs
Leonard re-signs with the Clippers in August 2021.
Sept 2021AspirationAspiration agreements
A 23-year, $382.5M jersey patch and founding arena partner agreement; a 23-year, $72M sustainability-services agreement for the Intuit Dome; a $50M personal Ballmer investment in Aspiration.
Oct 25, 2021AspirationZucker raises the idea
At her suggestion, Zucker meets Sanberg and raises sponsor-player endorsements, using Leonard and another player as examples.
Oct 27, 2021AspirationSanberg asks for help
Zucker told investigators she said the rules prevented her from assisting. The report finds that in the same conversation she offered to enlist a business agent under Clippers retention.
Oct 28, 2021Aspiration"Aspiration and Kawhi"
Minutes after speaking with Zucker, the agent emails colleagues the terms: $5M cash plus $7M stock per year for four years. The report concludes Zucker conveyed those terms.
Nov 3-4, 2021AspirationTerm sheet goes through the club
The agent sends Zucker the term sheet ("Let me know your thoughts"). Next day, after a call with her: "I reviewed with club to get input," three revisions, then to Sanberg: "Gillian shared with me that you guys spoke."
Nov 5, 2021AspirationThe "introduction" email
Zucker's formal intro of Robertson to Sanberg, written to appear responsive to Aspiration's interest. The report finds it was drafted "solely for record-making purposes."
Dec 2021AspirationAspiration's executives balk
"Kawhi, who is not a big name." Sanberg tells them the Clippers are asking and will provide additional business to offset the cost. "We should be fine if it's cashflow neutral."
Jan 2022AspirationForum term sheet: "$7M back in business"
According to Sanberg, he tells Zucker there is no Kawhi deal without business back. The first Forum draft carries a "Business Back Opporutnities" heading, to be filled in by Eric Chan.
Feb 2022AspirationFrank intervenes
Frankel texts Frank that Aspiration is unresponsive. Frank asks Zucker to step in. Frankel: "we are engaged again. Thank you."
March 2022Aspiration"Super shady"
Sonnenberg to Chan: "I never wanted this Forum deal. Always knew it was super shady."
March 30-31, 2022AspirationSanberg's ultimatum
"ZERO CHANCE there is a Kawhi deal if this isn't fixed." Chan to Ballmer's office: "Steve knows that when this deal blows up, Joe is calling Kawhi."
April 2022AspirationBallmer approves the Forum Agreement
Ballmer told investigators he was aware of Sanberg's threat; the record reflects he personally approved the agreement. The report finds this an act of facilitation that violates the rules. The Leonard-Aspiration agreement was completed; the report does not give its signing date.
Sept 3, 2025InvestigationPablo Torre Finds Out
The episode reveals the Aspiration deal, asserts it was never announced and involved no services, and airs an anonymous ex-employee saying its purpose was to circumvent the cap. The NBA hires Wachtell Lipton shortly after.
Sept 5, 2025InvestigationBallmer's TV defense
"We can introduce our sponsors to our athletes, we just can't be involved." "They were off to the races on their own. We weren't involved."
Aug 17, 2026InvestigationClippers statement
"Making introductions between players and team partners is both an ordinary practice by NBA teams and a common request of players and representatives."
Sept 2, 2026InvestigationSummary report issued
Findings against the Clippers, Ballmer, Zucker, Frank, Leonard and Robertson. Penalties left to the NBA. Investigators say they are still receiving information "as recently as this week."
