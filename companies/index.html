What the report says about each

LA ClippersTeam
125 mentions

Prior offender (2015, $250K fine). Received the NBA's circumvention training on December 4, 2019. Investigators describe the team's counsel as at times "adversarial or obfuscatory." The report rejects the implication in the team's August 17, 2026 statement that intros at a player's request are permitted.

Aspiration PartnersSponsor / endorser
62 mentions

Bankrupt sustainability company. September 2021: a 23-year, $382.5M sponsorship agreement, a 23-year, $72M sustainability-services agreement for the Intuit Dome, and a $50M personal Ballmer investment. The $48M Kawhi endorsement followed. Cooperated through its bankruptcy trustee.

NBALeague
50 mentions

Hired Wachtell Lipton and imposed no scope or timeline limits. Decides penalties separately. Its rules guidance explicitly lists a team recommending a player to a sponsor as prohibited conduct.

DaktronicsVendor / endorser
35 mentions

Scoreboard maker competing for the Intuit Dome contract. Told it was the preferred provider, then asked for a "spend back" that Zucker suggested could take the form of a Kawhi endorsement, at terms a senior Clippers executive specified. Substantial cooperation.

The ForumVenue
20 mentions

Inglewood arena Ballmer acquired in May 2020, overseen by Zucker. The $7M-a-year sustainability agreement (April 2022) is what Sanberg demanded before Aspiration would sign Kawhi. The team's consultant told investigators the Clippers gave him a $28M budget for the Forum's emissions, not the other way around.

Boingo WirelessVendor / endorser
14 mentions

Wireless provider. Multi-million dollar endorsement in July or August 2020 plus a consulting agreement with the Clippers. Initially cooperated, then supplied information investigators found not credible and stopped.

Lockton InsuranceVendor / endorser
14 mentions

Insurance broker. Endorsement signed in July or August 2020 plus a consulting agreement with the Clippers. Refused to cooperate.

Wachtell LiptonInvestigators
6 mentions

73 interviews of 60 people, more than 200,000 pages of documents. Says information is still arriving and it will supplement its findings as appropriate.

Intuit DomeVenue
4 mentions

The Clippers' new arena. Its scoreboard RFP is the lever the report describes in the Daktronics deal; Aspiration held founding arena partner status.

NBPAUnion
2 mentions

Provided counsel for Leonard during the investigation.

KL2 LBS LLCLeonard entity
1 mentions

Members: Leonard and Robertson. Articles filed June 9, 2020, the day of Zucker's second intro email. The report notes this was before any negotiations could have begun if the Clippers' stated introduction chronology were genuine. Counterparty on the Boingo, Daktronics and Lockton agreements.
